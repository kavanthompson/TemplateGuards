# C-Spine XR Fallback Template (Cervical Trauma)

**Indication:** [trauma / fall / MVA / neck pain]

**Technique:** Standard multi-view cervical spine radiographs (AP, lateral, open-mouth odontoid ± oblique).

**Comparison:** [prior / none]

**Findings:**
- Alignment: Normal cervical lordosis preserved (or straightened / reversed). No anterolisthesis or retrolisthesis.
- Prevertebral soft tissues: Within normal limits (C2–C4 ≤6 mm; C5–C7 ≤22 mm adult).
- Bones: No acute fracture, cortical disruption, or focal bone loss. Odontoid intact. Lateral masses symmetric.
- Disc spaces: Preserved. No significant narrowing.
- Soft tissues: No radiopaque foreign body.

**Trauma Checklist:**
- [ ] Atlanto-dens interval (ADI) ≤3 mm adult
- [ ] Lateral mass alignment / pillar view if available
- [ ] Facet joints congruent
- [ ] Spinous process alignment (posterior cervical line)
- [ ] Prevertebral soft-tissue swelling

**Impression:**
1. No acute fracture or malalignment of the cervical spine.

---
**ABR Pearl (toggle):** NEXUS / Canadian C-spine rules guide imaging necessity. Secondary signs of instability: >3.5 mm translation, >11° angulation, or widened interspinous distance. Always clear the cervicothoracic junction (C7–T1).
