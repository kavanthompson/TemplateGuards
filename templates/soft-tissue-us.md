# Soft-Tissue Ultrasound Fallback Template

**Indication:** [focal pain / swelling / redness / concern for abscess]

**Technique:** Grayscale and color Doppler ultrasound of the [site].

**Comparison:** [prior / none]

**Findings:**
- Skin/subcutaneous fat: [normal thickness / edema / cobblestoning].
- Collection: [none / hypoechoic or complex collection measuring __ x __ x __ cm].
- Drainability: [no discrete drainable collection / collection with posterior acoustic enhancement, amenable to aspiration if clinically indicated].
- Doppler: [no internal flow to suggest pseudoaneurysm or solid mass / peripheral hyperemia only].
- Foreign body: [none identified / echogenic focus with shadowing].
- Deeper structures: [fascia and muscle unremarkable / do not extend to bone cortex on this limited exam].

**Checklist (ED soft-tissue US):**
- [ ] Cellulitis cobblestoning vs discrete collection
- [ ] Compressibility and posterior acoustic enhancement
- [ ] Internal Doppler flow (exclude pseudoaneurysm / vascular malformation)
- [ ] Foreign body
- [ ] Depth relative to fascia (superficial vs deeper extension)

**Impression:**
1. No discrete drainable fluid collection. Findings compatible with soft-tissue edema / cellulitis if clinically correlated.

---
**ABR Pearl (toggle):** Cellulitis = subcutaneous cobblestoning and hyperemia without a drainable collection. Abscess = complex fluid with posterior enhancement, often peripheral hyperemia and no internal vascularity. Internal flow raises pseudoaneurysm or solid mass, not simple abscess.
