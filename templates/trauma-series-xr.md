# Portable Trauma-Series Radiograph Fallback Template

**Indication:** [blunt trauma / MVC / fall / trauma activation]

**Technique:** Portable AP chest, AP pelvis, and lateral cervical spine radiographs.

**Comparison:** [prior / none]

**Findings:**

Chest:
- Lungs: [clear / contusion / pneumothorax — check apices and costophrenic angles].
- Pleura: [no pleural line / visceral pleural line with absent peripheral markings].
- Mediastinum: [normal width / widened — clinical correlation for aortic injury; portable AP overcalls].
- Bones: [no acute rib, clavicle, or shoulder fracture / fracture of ___].
- Tubes/lines: [ETT __ cm above carina / central line / chest tube / none].

Pelvis:
- Rings: [intact / disruption of anterior or posterior ring].
- Hips: [concentric / dislocation or femoral neck fracture].
- Sacrum/SI joints: [symmetric / diastasis or vertical shear].

Cervical spine (lateral):
- Alignment: [normal lordosis / anterior or posterior translation].
- Prevertebral soft tissues: [normal / widened].
- Vertebral bodies and dens: [no fracture or height loss / fracture of ___].
- Adequacy: [C7–T1 visualized / inadequate — CT cervical spine recommended if not cleared clinically].

**Checklist (trauma series):**
- [ ] Apical pneumothorax and deep sulcus on supine CXR
- [ ] Mediastinal width is limited on portable AP
- [ ] Pelvic ring disruption, hip dislocation, femoral neck fracture
- [ ] C-spine adequacy to C7–T1; do not clear an inadequate film
- [ ] Support devices and unexpected free air

**Impression:**
1. No pneumothorax, no displaced pelvic ring injury, and no cervical fracture on this portable trauma series. Cervical spine clearance is incomplete if C7–T1 is not visualized.

---
**ABR Pearl (toggle):** Supine trauma CXR undercalls pneumothorax (look for deep sulcus) and overcalls mediastinal widening. Pelvic ring injuries are unstable if both anterior and posterior arches are disrupted. A lateral C-spine that does not include C7–T1 is inadequate; CT is the standard when clinical clearance fails.
