# Non-Contrast Abdomen XR Fallback Template

**Indication:** [pain / obstruction / constipation / free air concern]

**Technique:** Supine ± upright (or left lateral decubitus) abdominal radiographs.

**Comparison:** [prior / none]

**Findings:**
- Gas pattern: Normal bowel gas distribution. No dilated small-bowel loops (>3 cm) or large-bowel loops (>6 cm).
- Air-fluid levels: None (or physiologic if upright).
- Free intraperitoneal air: None under diaphragm / over liver (upright / decubitus).
- Soft tissues / organs: Non-specific. No abnormal calcifications (renal, biliary, vascular, appendicolith).
- Bones: Visualized portions unremarkable.

**Secondary Signs Checklist:**
- [ ] Rigler (double-wall) sign / continuous diaphragm sign (free air)
- [ ] String-of-pearls / step-ladder (SBO)
- [ ] Coffee-bean / bird-beak (volvulus)
- [ ] Appendicolith or other pathognomonic calcifications
- [ ] Soft-tissue mass effect or displacement of bowel

**Impression:**
1. No radiographic evidence of bowel obstruction or free intraperitoneal air.

---
**ABR Pearl (toggle):** Upright or left-lateral decubitus is required to exclude free air. Secondary signs of SBO (string-of-pearls) or volvulus raise urgency; CT is next step for most positive or equivocal cases.
